# VELMORA
## Platform
Web, mobile and desktop.
## Purpose
A completely fresh Nigerian fashion storefront for garment discovery, styling and order requests. Preserve the user's 23-part feature specification. New project and new layout; no old Velmora source.
## Identity
VELMORA. Editorial luxury selected by the user. Original Nigerian fashion photographs.
## Boundaries
Guest checkout records an order request without taking payment. Account saved pieces and orders use platform persistence. Browser bag and wardrobe preferences stay on the device. Uploaded customer photographs remain local; comparison is a side-by-side preview rather than generated try-on. Approved reviews only. Administration requires a signed-in authorized owner.
